Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Yako by the Sea (“Yako by the Sea,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a free, ad‑free educational game for young children, created by Jacob “Yako” Tanzer in Carmel‑by‑the‑Sea, California, and available at yakobythesea.com (the “Service”). We care deeply about the privacy of the children and families who use it. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Service handles, how it is stored, the third parties involved, our use of artificial intelligence, and your choices. Please read it together with our Terms of Use.

1. The short version

  • The game is designed to work with as little personal information as possible.
  • By default, the small amount of data the game uses (a player’s first name or nickname, learning progress, and preferences) is stored only on your own device in your browser — not on our servers.
  • We do not show ads, we do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not use children’s data for behavioral advertising.
  • Some artwork and voice narration in the game were created using AI tools during development. The game does not send your information to AI systems while you play.
  • A parent or guardian can delete all locally‑stored data at any time (see “Your choices and rights”).

2. Information we collect

Information you provide. To personalize the game, a child (with a parent’s help) may enter a first name or nickname. You are not required to enter a real name — a nickname works fine, and we ask you not to enter full names, contact details, or other identifying information.

Gameplay information stored on your device. The game saves the following in your browser’s local storage so progress isn’t lost: the chosen name/nickname, stars and level progress, lesson statistics (such as best times and play counts), stickers earned, daily‑streak information, and preferences (language, narrator voice, sound settings, and reading‑help options). This information stays on your device unless you choose to sign in for cross‑device sync (below).

Optional account and cloud sync. The game may offer an optional “Continue with Google” or “Continue with Facebook” feature so a parent can save progress across devices. If you choose to use it, you sign in with an adult’s existing account through that provider, and your progress may be stored in a cloud database (Google Firebase). This feature is optional and is not required to play. This should be set up and used by a parent or guardian, not a child.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider and any analytics or content‑delivery services we use may automatically receive basic technical information (such as IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, and pages viewed) to deliver and secure the Service and understand general, aggregate usage. Where we use analytics, we aim to use privacy‑friendly, cookieless tools that do not build advertising profiles.

3. Cookies and local storage

The core game does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It uses your browser’s local storage (a place on your own device) to save your progress and settings, and a service worker so the game can load quickly and work offline. Third‑party services listed below may set their own cookies or receive technical data as described in their policies. You can clear local storage and offline data at any time through your browser settings.

4. Third‑party services and data processors

We use a small number of third parties to run the Service. Each processes only the limited data needed for its function and under its own privacy policy:

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc. / Microsoft) — hosts and delivers the website; may log basic request/technical data.
  • Google Fonts (Google LLC) — delivers the fonts used by the game; may receive your IP address when fonts load.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. — may provide content delivery, security, and privacy‑friendly, cookieless web analytics.
  • Google Firebase and Google Sign‑In (Google LLC) — used only if you choose the optional sign‑in/cloud‑sync feature, to authenticate and store your saved progress.
  • Facebook Login (Meta Platforms, Inc.) — used only if you choose to sign in with Facebook for the optional cloud‑sync feature.

We may add, remove, or change providers over time and will update this list. We do not sell your personal information to these or any other parties.

5. Our use of artificial intelligence (AI)

We want to be transparent about AI. Some of the game’s artwork, background scenes, decorative images, and voice narration were generated with AI tools (for example, image‑ and audio‑generation services) as part of creating the game. This means certain characters, scenes, and spoken lines you see and hear were produced with AI assistance. To be clear: this AI generation happened during development to make the game’s content, and the game does not transmit your personal information, voice, image, or gameplay to AI systems while you play. AI‑generated content may occasionally contain imperfections; it is provided for learning and entertainment.

6. Children’s privacy (COPPA & GDPR‑K)

The Service is intended for young children and is meant to be used with the involvement of a parent or guardian. We designed it to collect as little information as possible and to keep data on the device by default. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child beyond the optional name/nickname used to personalize play, and we do not condition participation on disclosing more information than is reasonably necessary.

Consistent with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU/UK GDPR provisions for children, any optional account creation or cloud sync is intended to be performed by a parent or guardian using their own account. Parents and guardians can review and delete the information stored for their child (see below). If you believe a child has provided us personal information without appropriate consent, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. How we use information

  • To provide the game, save progress, and remember preferences.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the Service and understand general, aggregate usage.
  • To provide optional cross‑device sync if you enable it.

We do not use personal information for targeted advertising, and we do not sell or rent it.

8. Data retention

Data stored on your device remains until you clear it (for example, by using “Switch player,” clearing your browser’s site data, or uninstalling the app). If you use the optional cloud‑sync feature, saved progress is retained until you delete it or ask us to delete it.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Delete local data: use “Switch player” in the game, or clear this site’s data in your browser settings.
  • Play without an account: the full game works without signing in.
  • Access/deletion requests: depending on where you live (e.g., under GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information. Contact us and we will help.

10. Security

The Service is served over HTTPS, and we take reasonable measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. International users

The Service is operated from the United States. If you access it from other regions, you understand that information may be processed in the United States and other countries where our providers operate.

12. Links to other sites

The Service may link to third‑party sites or services that we do not control. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the “Last updated” date above, and significant changes will be reflected on this page. Your continued use of the Service after changes means you accept the updated policy.

14. Contact us

Questions or requests about privacy? Contact us at privacy@yakobythesea.com.

Yako by the Sea is a free, ad‑free educational game made with love for kids and families. If anything here is unclear, please reach out.
